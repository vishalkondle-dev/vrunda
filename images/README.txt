Vrunda's photos live in this folder.

The page reads the filenames from the CONFIG block near the top of
index.html -- heroPhoto, bandPhotos, galleryPhotos and familyPhotos.

Currently placed:
  hero         hero.jpeg              -- pink/orange traditional outfit
  newborn band newborn-1.jpeg, newborn-2.jpeg -- the two wide sleeping shots
  gallery (9)  gallery-1.jpeg .. gallery-9.jpeg -- the year in order
  family (3)   family-1.jpeg .. family-3.jpeg
  unused       unused-1.jpeg         -- not shown on the page

To add or reorder photos, edit those arrays. Portrait pictures suit
the arch frames (3:4) and the family cards (4:5); landscape suits the
newborn band (16:9). Any filename that cannot be found turns into a
decorative placeholder instead of a broken image.
